Kundli Matching for Marriage

A traditional comparison of two birth charts to support a family’s marriage decision.

What is Kundli matching?

Kundli matching compares the birth charts of two people before marriage. Many families follow the Ashtakoota (Guna Milan) method. [Confirm with Pandit Ji whether Guna Milan is offered]

It depends on accurate birth details for both people, especially the exact time and place of birth.

The consultation process

You send both sets of birth details. Pandit Ji’s team contacts you to confirm and explain the next steps. Matching offers traditional guidance. It does not guarantee any marriage outcome.

Questions about Kundli matching

How does traditional Kundli matching work?

Two birth charts are compared according to Vedic tradition, often using the Ashtakoota (Guna Milan) method, to inform a marriage discussion. It is guidance, not a guarantee.

What details are needed for Kundli matching?

Name, date of birth, exact time of birth and place of birth for both people, plus a contact number.
