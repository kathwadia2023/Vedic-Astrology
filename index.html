Frequently asked questions

What is a handwritten Vedic Kundli?

A handwritten Vedic Kundli is a birth chart drawn and written by hand by an astrologer from a person’s date, time and place of birth, following the traditional Vedic method.

What birth details are required to prepare a Kundli?

Full name, date of birth, exact time of birth and place of birth, plus a contact number and a complete postal address for delivery.

How can I order a handwritten Kundli?

Fill in the enquiry form on the Vedic Kundli page. Pandit Ji’s team confirms your details and the next steps with you.

Is the Kundli prepared manually?

Yes. Every Kundli is prepared personally by Pandit Ji. It is not generated by software and is not an instant download.
